How to change pitch without speeding up the track

  1. Open your audio

    Pick a voice recording or a track you want to transpose.

  2. Adjust the semitones

    A negative value lowers the pitch. Try +12 to go up a full octave.

  3. Listen and save

    Listen to the result and download the WAV when it sounds right.

Frequently asked questions

Does the duration change?

The WAV keeps the original track length. The processing changes pitch while keeping the tempo.

Why does my voice sound different?

Transposition shifts formants too. Large changes can make a voice sound less natural, so try two or three semitones first.

Is my file uploaded?

The file is processed in your browser. You don’t need an account.
